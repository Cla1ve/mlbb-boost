PassiveNinjutsu: Petal Barrage

Hanabi's Basic Attacks and skills launch Petal Blades upon hitting a target, dealing 30% damage and bouncing up to 4 times to nearby enemies. Petal Blades launched by Basic Attacks inherit Attack Effects.
If bounces from the same Basic Attack or skill hit the same target multiple times, subsequent bounces deal 30% less damage.

When the shield is active, she gains 20% Movement Speed and 25% Attack Speed, and 20% of her damage dealt to enemy heroes is added to this shield (only 10% of the damage dealt to non-hero units is added). Skill 2Ninjutsu: Soul Scroll
Cooldown 8 Mana Cost: 50 s

Hanabi fires the energy Kunai in the targeted direction, dealing 350 (+80% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage to enemies along the way and reducing their Movement Speed by 60% for 1s. Enemy units hit will also be marked, allowing Hanabi's next Bounce Damage from Basic Attacks to deal 30 (+100% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage on them with no damage decay.

UltimateForbidden Jutsu: Higanbana
Cooldown 40 Mana Cost: 120 s

Hanabi throws Higanbana in the targeted direction, dealing 300 (+50% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage to the first enemy hero hit and immobilizing them for 0.8s.
