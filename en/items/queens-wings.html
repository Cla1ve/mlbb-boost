How much does Queen's Wings cost and what does it build from?

2,250 gold. Parts: Expert Gloves (500), Ares Belt (700) and Hero's Ring (450); assembly adds 600 gold.

+750 HP, +30 Adaptive Attack, +10% Cooldown Reduction, +10% Spell Vamp. Demonize: when HP drops below 40%, gain 30% Damage Reduction for 3s and reduce skill cooldowns by 2s. 60s cooldown. Defiance: for every 1% HP lost, damage increases by 0.25%, up to 15%.
