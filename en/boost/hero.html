  1. 01

    Choose the hero and goal

    In the calculator pick the Hero format, your rank now and your goal.

    Name the hero and agree the start time. The hero must be on the account.

    Get your rank

    The booster plays only that hero, and progress shows in the bot.

Frequently asked questions

What is an MMR boost on a hero?

The booster raises your account rank playing only the hero you choose. That hero’s rating (MMR) grows with the stars.

How much does a hero boost cost?

From 65 ₽ per star in low ranks to 175 ₽ in Mythical Immortal. The calculator shows your route price.

Which heroes can be boosted?

Any hero on the account. Over the year customers ordered 182 different heroes, most often Angela, Selena and Lunox.

Is a hero leaderboard place guaranteed?

No. Hero rating depends on match results, so we do not promise a server or country leaderboard place.

How is a hero boost different from a role boost?

In a role boost the booster may use different heroes of one role. In a hero boost the whole route is one hero.
