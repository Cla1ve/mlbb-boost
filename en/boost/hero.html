Hero boost in Mobile Legends: rank and hero rating

A hero boost is a ranked route that the booster plays only on the hero you choose. Your account stars and that hero’s rating grow while your statistics stay on your main. Prices start at 65 ₽ per star; Mythic ranks cost 145 ₽–175 ₽.

The rate is per star and depends on the rank. Prices come from the main price list as of 22 November 2026; account and role rates are shown for comparison.

Our most popular format

Since November 2025 we have completed 816 hero orders — more than any other format — on 182 different heroes. The most ordered: Angela (51 orders), Selena (34), Lunox (30), Hayabusa and Fanny (23 each), Harley (22), Chou (21), Carmilla and Leomord (20 each), Xavier and Valentina (18 each). How an order works

  • Enter the hero, your rank and your goal in the calculator to see the price.
  • Confirm the order and start time in the Telegram bot. The hero must be on the account.
  • The booster plays only that hero; you can follow progress in the bot.

A place in the hero leaderboard for a server or country is not guaranteed: hero rating depends on match results and game rules. Read the security recommendations before sharing an account.

Hero, role or account boost

If only the rank matters, an account boost is cheaper. If you want to keep role statistics clean, choose a role boost. If you need a specific hero’s rating, choose this format. For Mythical Glory and Immortal stars, compare formats on the Mythic boost page.

Frequently asked questions

What is a hero boost in MLBB?

The booster raises your account rank playing only the hero you choose. That hero’s rating grows together with the stars.

How much does a hero boost cost?

From 65 ₽ per star in low ranks to 175 ₽ per star in Mythical Immortal. The calculator shows the exact route price.

Which heroes can be boosted?

Any hero on the account. Over the year customers ordered 182 different heroes; Angela, Selena and Lunox most often.

Is a hero leaderboard place guaranteed?

No. Hero rating depends on match results and game rules, so a server or country leaderboard place is not promised.

How is a hero boost different from a role boost?

In a role boost the booster may use different heroes of one role. In a hero boost the whole route is played on one chosen hero.
