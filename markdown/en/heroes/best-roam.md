## Frequently asked questions

Who is the best roamer in MLBB right now?

Marcel: tier S, 57.0% win rate, banned in 70.0% of Mythical Glory games over 7 days. Kalea and Carmilla follow. Data as of 25 November 2026.

Which of the roamers should a beginner pick?

Carmilla, Belerick and Tigreal are the easiest to play: their difficulty on the in-game hero card is 10, 10 and 10 out of 100. Easy heroes of every role are on a [separate page](https://boostmlbb.ru/en/heroes/for-beginners.html).

Which roamers get banned most?

Marcel (70.0%), Belerick (56.6%) and Carmilla (37.3%). That is the share of Mythical Glory games over 7 days in which the hero was banned.

How many roamers are there in Mobile Legends?

34 of 133 heroes as of 25 November 2026. The list has every hero that plays roam, according to the game.

Which items do roamers build most often?

The most common builds most often include Dominance Ice (16 of 34), Thunder Belt (9 of 34) and Fleeting Time (9 of 34). Every hero’s full build is on its page, in the Build tab.
