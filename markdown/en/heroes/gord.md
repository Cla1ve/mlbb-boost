## Skills

Level-up priority · Mystic Gush → Mystic Injunction → Mystic Projectile

![](/images/mlbb/skills/gord-0.webp)Passive**Mystic Favor**

After Gord hits the same target 4 times with skills in quick succession, his next skill deals 120 (+8\*Hero Level) (+40% Total Magic Power) extra True Damage and slows them by 20% for 1s (the slow effect stacks up to 2 times).

![](/images/mlbb/skills/gord-1.webp)Skill 1**Mystic Projectile**

**Cooldown 8 Mana Cost: 80 s**

Gord conjures a Mystic Orb that bounces in the target direction and explodes on the first enemy hit, dealing 225 (+65% Total Magic Power) Magic Damage to enemies in the area, while stunning them and revealing their locations for 1s.

![](/images/mlbb/skills/gord-2.webp)Skill 2**Mystic Injunction**

**Cooldown 8 Mana Cost: 60 s**

Gord creates an energy field in the target area, dealing 30 (+10% Total Magic Power) Magic Damage every 0.25s to enemies within (up to 16 times).

![](/images/mlbb/skills/gord-3.webp)Ultimate**Mystic Gush**

**Cooldown 44 Mana Cost: 150 s**

Gord unleashes an energy beam in the target direction, dealing 90 (+32% Total Magic Power) Magic Damage to enemies hit every 0.2s (up to 16 times).
The direction of the beam can be adjusted using the Joystick.
