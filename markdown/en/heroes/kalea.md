Passive

### Surge of Life

After casting a skill, Kalea creates a Water Zone on the ground. When Kalea casts a skill within this zone, she absorbs it to enhance her next 3 Basic Attacks with different effects and gains 60% Movement Speed for 1s:

**First Basic Attack:** Kalea dashes to the target, dealing (+100% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage.

**Second Basic Attack:** Kalea unleashes a flurry of attacks, dealing 140 (+8 × hero level) (+100% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage and recovering (+12% Max HP) (+80% Total Magic Power) HP to herself and the nearby ally with the lowest HP.

**Third Basic Attack:** Kalea strikes around the target with her tail, dealing 120 (+12 × hero level) (+100% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage and creating a new Water Zone. (Enhanced Basic Attacks are not affected by Attack Speed.)

Control effects can affect Kalea during the third Enhanced Basic Attack, but cannot interrupt it.

![](/images/mlbb/skills/kalea-1.webp)

Skill 1

### Wavebreaker

Cooldown **3.5 s**

Kalea slams her fist into the ground, creating a wave that deals 200 (+5% Max HP) Physical Damage to nearby enemies and slows them by 40% for 1.5s.

![](/images/mlbb/skills/kalea-2.webp)

Skill 2

### Tidal Strike

Cooldown **16 s**

Kalea charges forward and body slams the first enemy hero she encounters, knocking them airborne for 0.6s (unaffected by Resilience) while dealing 150 (+60% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage.

If she collides with an obstacle or touches the edge of a Water Zone during her charge, Kalea will leap into the air. Cast the skill again to slam down at a target location, dealing 150 (+60% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage and knocking enemies airborne for 0.6s (unaffected by Resilience).

![](/images/mlbb/skills/kalea-3.webp)

Ultimate

### Tsunami Slam

Cooldown **56 s**

Kalea grabs an enemy hero with her tail and slams them down in the Joystick's direction (drag the Joystick to change landing spot). She gains Control Immunity during this process. Upon landing, deals 300 (+14% Max HP) Physical Damage to the target and nearby enemies.
