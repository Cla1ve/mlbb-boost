## Skills

Level-up priority · Holy Baptism → Light of Retribution → Holy Healing

![](/images/mlbb/skills/rafaela-0.webp)Passive**Divine Resurrection**

Rafaela can cast a special skill every 10s. After channeling for 2.5s, she instantly resurrects an ally and increases their Movement Speed by 30% for 5s (taking damage from heroes and Legend Creeps interrupts channeling and puts the skill on base cooldown).
This skill's cooldown is increased by 600% of the target's remaining respawn time, capped at 240s. This skill is not affected by Cooldown Reduction.

![](/images/mlbb/skills/rafaela-1.webp)Skill 1**Light of Retribution**

**Cooldown 5 Mana Cost: 45 s**

Rafaela strikes the three nearest enemies with Light of Retribution, dealing them 350 (+120% Total Magic Power) Magic Damage, briefly revealing their positions, and slowing them by 40% for 1.5s. Enemies hit by Light of Retribution again within 6s will take 20% extra damage (this effect stacks up to 3 times).

![](/images/mlbb/skills/rafaela-2.webp)Skill 2**Holy Healing**

**Cooldown 10.5 Mana Cost: 75 s**

Rafaela calls upon Holy Light, recovering 100 (+35% Total Magic Power) HP to nearby allied heroes, plus an additional 150 (+65% Total Magic Power) HP to herself and the most injured allied hero in range. She also increases the Movement Speed of nearby allied heroes by 30% and grants Slow Immunity for 1s.
Every 10 increases the Movement Speed bonus by 1%.

![](/images/mlbb/skills/rafaela-3.webp)Ultimate**Holy Baptism**

**Cooldown 38 Mana Cost: 120 s**

Rafaela unleashes the true power of Holy Light in the target direction, dealing 460 (+120% Total Magic Power) Magic Damage to enemies in a line and stunning them for 1.2s.
