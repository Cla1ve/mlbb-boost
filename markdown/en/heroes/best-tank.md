## Frequently asked questions

Who is the best tank in MLBB right now?

Masha: tier S, 58.8% win rate, banned in 67.2% of Mythical Glory games over 7 days. Carmilla and Belerick follow. Data as of 25 November 2026.

Which of the tanks should a beginner pick?

Carmilla, Belerick and Tigreal are the easiest to play: their difficulty on the in-game hero card is 10, 10 and 10 out of 100. Easy heroes of every role are on a [separate page](https://boostmlbb.ru/en/heroes/for-beginners.html).

Which tanks get banned most?

Masha (67.2%), Belerick (56.6%) and Carmilla (37.3%). That is the share of Mythical Glory games over 7 days in which the hero was banned.

How many tanks are there in Mobile Legends?

25 of 133 heroes as of 25 November 2026. The list has every hero with the Tank role.

Which items do tanks build most often?

The most common builds most often include Dominance Ice (14 of 25), Thunder Belt (7 of 25) and Oracle (7 of 25). Every hero’s full build is on its page, in the Build tab.
