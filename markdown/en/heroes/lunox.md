## Lunox skills

Official skill descriptions from MOONTON with cooldowns and tags.

Level-up priority: **Order & Chaos** → **Starlight Pulse** → **Chaos Assault** → **Cosmic Fission**

-   ![](/images/mlbb/skills/lunox-0.webp)

    **Dreamland Twist**Passive

    Lunox is twisted by the powers of Chaos and Order.
    When Lunox uses Power of Order, she gains 10% Spell Vamp. When she uses Power of Chaos, she gains 10% Magic Penetration.

-   ![](/images/mlbb/skills/lunox-1.webp)

    **Starlight Pulse**Skill 1CD 2 Mana Cost: 30 s

    Lunox summons a rain of starlight upon nearby enemies, dealing 200 (+110% Total Magic Power) Magic Damage. The starlight then returns to Lunox, each recovering 75 (+30% Total Magic Power) HP to her (doubled when hitting enemy heroes).
    This skill shares a cooldown with Chaos Assault. It grants Power of Order and ends the Power of Chaos state.

-   ![](/images/mlbb/skills/lunox-2.webp)

    **Chaos Assault** Skill 2CD 2 Mana Cost: 30 s

    Lunox unleashes Chaos Energy at a target enemy, dealing 250 (+120% Total Magic Power) (+ 3.5% target's ) Magic Damage (+ 2.1% against creeps).
    This skill shares its cooldown with Starlight Pulse. Using it grants Power of Chaos and ends the Power of Order state.

-   ![](/images/mlbb/skills/lunox-3.webp)

    **Cosmic Fission**Skill 3CD 10 Mana Cost: 70 s

    Lunox channels the power of Order and Chaos in the target direction, dealing 300 (+150% Total Magic Power) Magic Damage to enemies in its path and slowing them by 40% for 2s.

-   ![](/images/mlbb/skills/lunox-4.webp)

    **Order & Chaos**UltimateCD 30 Mana Cost: 120 s

    Lunox is twisted by the powers of Chaos and Order.
    When Power of Order is dominant, she can cast Brilliance to become invincible and deal continuous damage to nearby enemies.
    When Power of Chaos is dominant, she can cast Darkening to blink in a target direction and greatly reduce the cooldown of Chaos Assault for a brief period.
