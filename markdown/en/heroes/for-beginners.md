## Frequently asked questions

Which hero is the easiest in Mobile Legends?

11 heroes have a difficulty of 10 out of 100. Among the easy ones, Marcel, Masha and Belerick win the most in Epic and Legend.

Which role is easiest for a beginner?

On average tanks (difficulty 38) and supports (43) are the easiest, assassins the hardest (59).

Which lane should a beginner pick?

Heroes of roam have the lowest average difficulty, 42 out of 100. Heroes of the jungle have the highest, 51.

Which heroes should a beginner avoid?

Those rated 80 out of 100 or more: there are 12 now, including Fanny, Paquito, Lunox and Kagura.
