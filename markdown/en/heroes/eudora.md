## Skills

Level-up priority · Thunder’s Wrath → Forked Lightning → Ball Lightning

![](/images/mlbb/skills/eudora-0.webp)Passive**Superconductor**

Eudora's skills inflict Superconductor for 5s on non-minion units hit and can trigger additional effects against enemies affected by Superconductor.

![](/images/mlbb/skills/eudora-1.webp)Skill 1**Forked Lightning**

**Cooldown 5 Mana Cost: 40 s**

Eudora casts Forked Lightning in a fan-shaped area, dealing 275 (+100% Total Magic Power) Magic Damage (increased to 200% against minions) to enemies within.
When hitting targets affected by Superconductor, Eudora forms a lightning chain with the target, gaining 40% Movement Speed during the chain for up to 1s.
The lightning chain deals 10 (+4% Total Magic Power) Magic Damage over time and an additional 275 (+100% Total Magic Power) Magic Damage when it ends. If this hit lands, it reduces this skill's cooldown by 50%.

![](/images/mlbb/skills/eudora-2.webp)Skill 2**Ball Lightning**

**Cooldown 11 Mana Cost: 80 s**

Eudora hurls an orb of lightning at the target enemy, reducing their by 10 for 1.8s, dealing 300 (+50% Total Magic Power) Magic Damage, and stunning them for 1s.
If the target is affected by Superconductor, the lightning orb reduces nearby enemies' , deals AOE damage centered on the target, and stuns all nearby enemies.

![](/images/mlbb/skills/eudora-3.webp)Ultimate**Thunder’s Wrath**

**Cooldown 32 Mana Cost: 130 s**

Eudora calls down lightning on the target area, dealing 540 (+145% Total Magic Power) Magic Damage to targets at the center. A second strike then deals 270 (+90% Total Magic Power) Magic Damage to enemies in the outer area.
When Thunder's Wrath hits a target affected by Superconductor, it triggers a Thunderburst at the target's location after a short delay, dealing 300 (+100% Total Magic Power) Magic Damage to them.
