## Skills

Level-up priority · Heavy Crossbow → Strafe → Force of The Queen

![](/images/mlbb/skills/irithel-0.webp)Passive**Jungle Heart**

Irithel and Leo's immaculate teamwork allows her to shoot while moving. Each Basic Attack will shoot 2 crossbow bolt(s) (but takes longer to refill) that deal (+55% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage per bolt (inherits a portion of Attack Effects).
Moving will build up Jungle Heart, up to a maximum of 10 units, and cause the next Basic Attack to unleash 1 additional bolt(s).

![](/images/mlbb/skills/irithel-1.webp)Skill 1**Strafe**

**Cooldown 10 s**

Irithel launches a volley of arrows on the target area, dealing 330 (+60% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage to enemies within and reducing their by 15 for 3s.

![](/images/mlbb/skills/irithel-2.webp)Skill 2**Force of The Queen**

**Cooldown 10 s**

Leo lets out a piercing roar, dealing 200 (+60% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage to nearby enemies and slowing them by 40% for 2s.

![](/images/mlbb/skills/irithel-3.webp)Ultimate**Heavy Crossbow**

**Cooldown 30 s**

Irithel commands Leo to leap in the target direction and empowers her crossbow for 10s.
During this time, she gains 30 Movement Speed, generates Jungle Heart faster, and fires heavy bolts with her Basic Attacks, dealing 100 (+65% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage to the target and nearby units.
