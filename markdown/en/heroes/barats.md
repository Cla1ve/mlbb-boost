## Skills

![](/images/mlbb/skills/barats-0.webp)Passive**Big Guy**![](/images/mlbb/skills/barats-1.webp)Skill 1**So-Called Teamwork**![](/images/mlbb/skills/barats-2.webp)Skill 2**Missile "Expert"**![](/images/mlbb/skills/barats-3.webp)Ultimate**Detona's Welcome**

Passive

### Big Guy

Detona gains a stack of Big Guy for 12s each time it or Barats damages an enemy with their skills (up to 10 stacks). Each stack of Big Guy increases Detona's size and grants 4 - 10 (scales with the level of Detona's Welcome). At max stacks, Detona gains 20% extra Resilience, and its Basic Attacks are replaced with Trample, which deals (+140% Total Physical Attack) (+3.5% Max HP) Physical Damage to enemies in a circular area, slows them by 40% for 0.2s, and refreshes the duration of Big Guy with each hit.

Skill 1

### So-Called Teamwork

Cooldown **7 Mana Cost: 25 s**

Detona spits contaminated oil in the target direction, dealing 60 (+7% Max HP) Physical Damage to enemies in a fan-shaped area. Barats then ignites the oil with a firecracker, dealing 120 (+180% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage to enemies in the area (only 50% damage to Creeps).

The area of effect of So-Called Teamwork increases with the number of Big Guy stacks.

Skill 2

### Missile "Expert"

Cooldown **11 Mana Cost: 70 s**

Barats fires two missiles at the target location, dealing 100 (+75% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage and slowing enemies by 40% for 1s. The missiles expel fumes from their exhausts upon landing, dealing 100 (+75% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage to enemies in a rectangular area and pushing them toward Barats.

Ultimate

### Detona's Welcome

Cooldown **50 Mana Cost: 100 s**

After a short delay, Detona charges in the target direction, dealing 120 (+70% Total Physical Attack) (+1.2% Max HP) Physical Damage to the first enemy hit, devouring and suppressing them for 1.2s, then spitting them out. If the spat‑out target hits an enemy hero or a wall, it triggers an explosion. The target and nearby enemies take an additional 180 (+105% Total Physical Attack) (+5% Max HP) Physical Damage and are stunned for 1s.

During the skill, Barats becomes immune to control effects. If the cast is canceled or the skill misses, 50% of the skill cooldown is refunded.

If Detona successfully devours an enemy, he immediately gains max stacks of Big Guy.
