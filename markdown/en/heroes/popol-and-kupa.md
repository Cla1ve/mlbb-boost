## Popol and Kupa skills

Official skill descriptions from MOONTON with cooldowns and tags.

Level-up priority: **We Are Angry!** → **Bite 'em, Kupa!** → **Kupa, Help!** → **Popol's Surprise**

-   ![](/images/mlbb/skills/popol-and-kupa-0.webp)

    **We Are Friends**Passive

    Kupa recovers 10% Max HP per second after not taking damage for 5s. When Kupa dies, Popol can Pray for 3s to revive it. Cooldown: 30s.
    Kupa has 3500 (+350\*Hero Level) Max HP and inherits 100% of Popol's other attributes and equipment effects.
    Kupa deals 77 (+3\*Hero Level) extra True Damage to creeps.

-   ![](/images/mlbb/skills/popol-and-kupa-1.webp)

    **Bite 'em, Kupa!**Skill 1CD 7 Mana Cost: 50 s

    Popol throws a spear at the target, signaling Kupa to attack and dealing 80 (+70% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage. Kupa then bites the target, dealing 40 (+40% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage and slowing them by 99% for 0.75s.
    While marked by the spear, whenever Popol or Kupa hits the target with a Basic Attack, both gain 7% Attack Speed and 3% Movement Speed for 4s (up to 3 stacks).
    Alpha Wolf Form: Kupa strikes fiercely, dealing 120 (+140% Total Physical Attack) Physical Damage to the target and nearby enemies and knocking them airborne for 0.5s.

-   ![](/images/mlbb/skills/popol-and-kupa-2.webp)

    **Kupa, Help!**Skill 2CD 10 Mana Cost: 50 s

    Popol calls Kupa back and gains a 240 (+200% Extra Physical Attack) shield. He can then ride Kupa for 1.5s, gaining 250 Movement Speed while mounted. Kupa also continuously recovers 20% of its Max HP.
    Popol can use a Basic Attack to end the Mounted state early. After returning, Kupa follows Popol until Popol attacks an enemy.
    Alpha Wolf Form: Shield effect increases to 125%, and Kupa recovers full HP while Mounted.

-   ![](/images/mlbb/skills/popol-and-kupa-3.webp)

    **Popol's Surprise**Skill 3CD 1.5 Mana Cost: 50 s

    Popol sets a trap. After a short delay, it immobilizes the enemy stepping on it for 1s. It then explodes to create a frost zone for 4s, which continuously slows enemies within by 20%.
    Popol gains a trap charge every 22s (up to 3 charges). Up to 4 traps can exist at the same time, each lasting up to 60s.

-   ![](/images/mlbb/skills/popol-and-kupa-4.webp)

    **We Are Angry!**UltimateCD 38 Mana Cost: 100 s

    Popol and Kupa enter the Enraged state for 9s.
    During this time, Kupa turns into Alpha Wolf Form.
