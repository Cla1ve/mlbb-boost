## Effect

Unique passive

### Destiny

Magic Damage to enemy heroes grants 4.5 (+0.5 × hero level) Hybrid Defense for 5s, up to 6 stacks (once every 0.4s).

Unique passive

### Gift

When HP drops below 50%, recover 15% HP over 3s; when Mana drops below 50%, restore 15% Mana over 3s. 60s cooldown.
