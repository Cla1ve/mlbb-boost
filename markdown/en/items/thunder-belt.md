## Effect

Unique passive

### Thunderbolt

Every 4s, the next Basic Attack deals 50 (+100% extra Physical Defense, +100% extra Magic Defense) True Damage to the target and nearby enemies and briefly slows them by 99%. Each hit on a hero permanently adds 1 Hybrid Defense. Halved for Marksmen and Mages.
