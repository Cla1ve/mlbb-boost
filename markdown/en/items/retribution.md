# Retribution

[Jungle](https://boostmlbb.ru/en/items/#jungle)Jungle blessing

## Options

![](/images/mlbb/blessings/flame-retribution.webp)

### Flame Retribution

Cast on an enemy hero, it deals 100 True Damage and steals 71–120 Physical Attack and Magic Power (scales with level) for 3s.

For duels: the enemy gets weaker while you get stronger.

![](/images/mlbb/blessings/ice-retribution.webp)

### Ice Retribution

Cast on an enemy hero, it deals 100 True Damage and steals 52–80 Movement Speed (scales with level) for 3s.

For chasing a target down or getting away.

![](/images/mlbb/blessings/bloody-retribution.webp)

### Bloody Retribution

Cast on an enemy hero, it deals 100 True Damage and steals HP equal to 300 + 24% of the caster’s extra HP for 3s.

For junglers who need to survive the fight.

## How it works

### Upgrade

Retribution is upgraded once creep kills, hero kills and assists add up to 5.

### Hunter

Boots with a jungle blessing: after damaging a creep, deal 40 (+3 × hero level) + 1% of Total HP as True Damage over 3s, doubled for non-Marksmen.

## Frequently asked questions

What Retribution options are there in MLBB?

There are 3: Flame Retribution, Ice Retribution and Bloody Retribution. Flame Retribution — for duels: the enemy gets weaker while you get stronger. Ice Retribution — for chasing a target down or getting away. Bloody Retribution — for junglers who need to survive the fight.
