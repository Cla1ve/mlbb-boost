## 1\. What are cookies

1.1. **Cookies** are small text files saved on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit websites.

1.2. Cookies allow the website to:

-   "Remember" you and your preferences
-   Ensure the functionality works correctly
-   Collect website usage statistics
-   Improve the user experience

1.3. Cookies do NOT contain viruses and cannot harm your device.

## 2\. Which cookies we use

2.1. The boostmlbb.ru website uses the following categories of cookies:

### Essential (technical) cookies

They ensure the basic operation of the site. Without them the site will not work correctly.

-   **Session cookies** preserve the session state during navigation
-   **Security cookies** protection against CSRF attacks
-   **Consent cookies** storing your consent to data processing

### Functional cookies

They improve the site by remembering your preferences.

-   **Language settings** remembering the selected language
-   **Theme** light/dark (if applicable)
-   **Recently viewed** history of viewed pages

### Analytics cookies

They collect anonymized website usage statistics.

-   **Yandex.Metrica** user behavior analysis
-   **Google Analytics** visit statistics

## 5\. Cookie retention periods

5.1. Cookies differ by retention period:

### Session cookies

Deleted automatically when the browser is closed.

### Persistent cookies

-   **Consent cookies:** 1 year
-   **Yandex.Metrica:** up to 2 years
-   **Google Analytics:** up to 2 years
-   **Functional cookies:** up to 1 year

5.2. You can delete cookies at any time via your browser settings.

## 6\. Managing cookies

6.1. You can manage cookies in several ways:

### Browser settings

Most browsers allow you to:

-   Block all cookies
-   Block third-party cookies
-   Delete cookies when closing the browser
-   View and delete individual cookies

### Instructions for popular browsers

-   **Google Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
-   **Mozilla Firefox:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
-   **Safari:** Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
-   **Microsoft Edge:** Settings → Cookies and site permissions
-   **Opera:** Settings → Advanced → Privacy → Cookies

### Disabling analytics

-   **Yandex.Metrica:** [Opt-out instructions](https://yandex.ru/support/metrica/general/opt-out.html)
-   **Google Analytics:** [Browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)

## 7\. Impact of disabling cookies

#### Warning

Disabling cookies may affect how the site works.

7.1. When cookies are disabled:

-   The site may not work correctly
-   Your preferences will not be saved
-   You will have to give consent again on every visit
-   Some features may be unavailable

7.2. We recommend allowing cookies for the best site experience.
