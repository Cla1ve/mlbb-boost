## 2\. Payment methods

2.1. The Service accepts payments using the following methods:

### Bank transfers (Russia)

-   **Faster Payments System (SBP)** — instant transfers by phone number
-   **Sberbank** — transfers by card or phone number
-   **Tinkoff** — transfers by card or phone number
-   **VTB** — transfers by card number
-   **Alfa-Bank** — transfers by card number
-   **MIR, Visa, Mastercard cards** — via transfer to a card

### Cryptocurrency

-   **USDT (Tether)** — TRC-20, ERC-20 networks
-   Other cryptocurrencies — by agreement

#### Important

All payments are made directly to the Operator or Provider. The Service does NOT store bank card data and does NOT process payments through its own acquiring.

## 3\. Payment security

3.1. **No storage of payment data:** We do not store bank card numbers, CVV codes, or online banking passwords. All payments are made through official bank apps or crypto wallets.

3.2. **Payment confirmation:** After making a payment, it is recommended to send a screenshot of the receipt to confirm the transaction.

3.3. **Fraud protection:**

-   Verification of the payment source
-   Verification of large transactions
-   Monitoring of suspicious activity

3.4. **What we store about payments:**

-   Date and time of payment
-   Payment amount
-   Payment method (without detailed credentials)
-   Transaction ID (if available)

3.5. **Retention period:** Financial transaction information is stored for 5 years in accordance with the requirements of Russian tax law.

## 4\. Protection of transmitted data

4.1. **SSL/TLS encryption:** The boostmlbb.ru website uses an SSL certificate that encrypts all data transmitted between the user's browser and the server.

#### How to check SSL

Make sure the browser address bar shows a padlock icon 🔒 and the address starts with **https://**

4.2. **Telegram:** All communication via Telegram is protected by end-to-end encryption (in secret chats) and client-server encryption (in regular chats).

4.3. **Protection against interception:**

-   Use of HTTPS on all pages
-   HSTS (HTTP Strict Transport Security)
-   Protection against MITM (Man-in-the-Middle) attacks

## 5\. Storage of game account credentials

#### Important information

Game account credentials (login, password) are confidential information and are processed with special care.

5.1. **Processing principles:**

-   **Minimization:** Data is requested only when necessary (for solo boosting)
-   **Restricted access:** Only the assigned Provider has access to the data
-   **Temporary storage:** Data is deleted after the order is completed
-   **Not shared with third parties:** Data is not sold or shared with outsiders

5.2. **Recommendations after a boost:**

-   Change your game account password
-   Check your account links (email, social media)
-   Enable two-factor authentication (if available)

5.3. **Retention period:** Game account credentials are deleted within 24 hours after the order is completed.

## 6\. Provider verification

6.1. All Providers (Boosters) undergo mandatory verification before being allowed to work:

### Verification stages

2.  **Test assignments:** Completing supervised test orders
3.  **Reputation check:** Analysis of reviews and work history
4.  **Identification:** Identity confirmation (Telegram account)

6.2. **Requirements for Providers:**

-   Mythic Glory rank or participation in MCC tournaments
-   A win rate of at least 85%

-   Signing a confidentiality agreement

6.3. **Monitoring:** Providers' work is constantly monitored. If violations are detected, they are removed from work.

## 7\. Fraud protection

7.1. The Operator applies a set of measures to prevent fraud:

### Technical measures

-   Logging of all actions

### Organizational measures

-   User verification for large orders
-   Dispute resolution via the support service

## 8\. What to do if your account is hacked

8.1. **Signs of a hacked game account:**

-   Inability to log in to the account
-   A changed linked email or phone number
-   Missing resources, items, or currency
-   Unauthorized purchases
-   Suspicious game sessions in the history

8.2. **Immediate actions:**

1.  **Change the password** — if you have access to the account
2.  **Check the links** — email, social media, phone
3.  **Contact the game's support** — Moonton Customer Service
4.  **Contact us** — if the hack occurred during the boost

8.3. **What our support service will do:**

-   Check the logs of interaction with the Provider
-   Establish the circumstances of the incident
-   Take action against the Provider (if at fault)
-   Compensate for the damage under the Refund Policy

8.4. **If the hack is not related to our Service:**

-   Contact Moonton Games support
-   Submit an account recovery request
-   If necessary — contact law enforcement authorities

## 9\. Security recommendations

9.1. **To protect your game account:**

-   Use a unique strong password (at least 12 characters)
-   Link the account to an email that only you control
-   Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)
-   Do not share data with outsiders
-   Change your password regularly
-   Do not use the same password for different services

9.2. **When ordering a boost:**

-   Order only through official channels (bot @cla1ve\_boost\_bot)
-   Do not click on suspicious links
-   Make sure you are communicating with the official account
-   Keep screenshots of correspondence and payments
-   After the boost — change your password

9.3. **To protect your finances:**

-   Do not share your CVV code or online banking passwords
-   Transfer money only to verified details
-   Keep payment receipts
-   If you receive suspicious requests — contact us
