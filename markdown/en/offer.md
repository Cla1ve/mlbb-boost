## 1\. Terms and definitions

**1.1. Agent** — a Sole Proprietor providing an automated platform (interface) for interaction between Customers and Providers.

**1.2. Principal (Customer)** — a natural person interested in receiving game services and using the Service to choose a Provider.

**1.3. Provider (Booster)** — a third party verified in the Agent's Service who directly provides game services to the Principal.

**1.4. Service** — a software system (Telegram bot) located at: [https://t.me/cla1ve\_boost\_bot?start=site](https://t.me/cla1ve_boost_bot?start=site).

## 2\. Subject of the agreement

**2.1.** The Agent provides the Principal with access to the Service containing a database of verified Providers and the technical means to choose a Provider to complete an order in online games.

**2.2.** The Agent is not the provider of the game service. The Agent acts as an information intermediary and a guarantor of the transaction's security. All obligations regarding the direct quality of gameplay rest with the Provider.

**2.3.** The transaction is deemed concluded under the terms of this Agreement from the moment the Principal starts using the Service and places an order.

## 3\. Financial terms and settlement procedure

**3.1.** The total cost of services is determined by the rates specified in the Service interface.

**3.2.** Settlements under the Agreement are made as follows: the Principal sends payment for the order directly to the Provider (or via a transit account), while the Provider instructs the Agent to withhold or transfer the Agent's fee for use of the Service.

**3.3.** The Agent's income is recognized only as the Agent's fee (commission) for providing access to the Service and technical support of the transaction. Funds due to the Provider are not the Agent's income.

## 4\. Rights and obligations of the parties

### 4.1. The Agent undertakes to:

-   **4.1.1.** Provide the Principal with access to the database of verified Providers.
-   **4.1.2.** Ensure the technical operability of the Service.
-   **4.1.3.** Act as a guarantor of the transaction's security: if the Provider fails to complete an order or completes it poorly, the Agent ensures a refund to the Principal out of the Provider's funds withheld or payable to the Provider, in accordance with the Refund Policy and Service rules.

### 4.2. The Principal undertakes to:

-   **4.2.1.** Independently choose a Provider and place an order via the Service interface.
-   **4.2.2.** Not log in to the game account or change credentials while the Provider is completing the order.
-   **4.2.3.** Provide the correct data the Provider needs to log in to the account (if required by the order terms).

## 5\. Liability of the parties

-   Blocking, banning, or other sanctions on the Principal's game account by the game administration. The Principal acknowledges the risks of granting access to third parties.
-   Technical failures on the side of the game developer's game servers.

**5.3.** The Provider's liability to the Principal is governed by the Service rules. The Agent assists in resolving disputes between the Principal and the Provider.

## 6\. Dispute resolution and refunds

**6.1.** Complaints about service quality are accepted via the contact details specified in the Service interface (the "Support" section or equivalent).

**6.2.** A refund or compensation is possible in the following cases:

-   The Provider did not start the order;
-   The order was not completed in full;
-   The order was completed poorly (in violation of the deadlines or terms agreed in the Service).

The decision on a refund or compensation is made by the Agent based on a review of logs and evidence provided by the parties.
