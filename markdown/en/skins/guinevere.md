## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Guinevere have?

10 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Daybreak Halo (3 October 2025).

Which Guinevere skins can you buy now?

Sakura Wishes (749 diamonds), Amethyst Dance (749 diamonds), Fleur de Bleau (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Guinevere skin?

Psion of Tomorrow, Supreme class, Legend series. Exchanged for Magic Crystals from the Magic Wheel. Limited skins come back in event draws from time to time.
