## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Esmeralda have?

8 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Astral Muse (3 April 2025).

Which Esmeralda skins can you buy now?

Blazing Shadow (899 diamonds), Lady Thief (749 diamonds), Ruddy Dusk (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Esmeralda skin?

The Foreseer, Grand class, Dawning Stars series. Released in the Dawning Stars event. Limited skins come back in event draws from time to time.
