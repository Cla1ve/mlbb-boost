## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Luo Yi have?

7 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Starborne Matron (14 July 2025).

Which Luo Yi skins can you buy now?

Starborne Matron (899 diamonds), Oracle of Sol (899 diamonds), Tenko (599 diamonds), Yin-yang Mage (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Luo Yi skin?

Elysium Guardian, Exquisite class, Collector series. The top prize of a Collector event draw, paid with diamonds. Limited skins come back in event draws from time to time.
