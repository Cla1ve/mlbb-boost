## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Vale have?

9 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Windborne Waltz (16 September 2026).

Which Vale skins can you buy now?

Keeper of the Winds (899 diamonds), Kannagi (599 diamonds), Cerulean Winds (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Vale skin?

Blizzard Storm, Grand class, Dawning Stars series. Released in the Dawning Stars event. Limited skins come back in event draws from time to time.
