## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Layla have?

12 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Prismatic Rhythm (9 October 2026).

Which Layla skins can you buy now?

Blazing Gun (899 diamonds), S.A.B.E.R. Breacher (899 diamonds), Green Flash (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Layla skin?

The Beacon, Supreme class, Legend Nexus Sea series. Released in the Legend Nexus Sea event. Limited skins come back in event draws from time to time.
