## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Diggie have?

5 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Underwood Steward (1 October 2024).

Which Diggie skins can you buy now?

Circus Clown (599 diamonds), Constellation (749 diamonds), Pigeoneer (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Diggie skin?

Fairytaler, Deluxe class, Epic series. Released in the skin shop during an event. Limited skins come back in event draws from time to time.
