## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Hanabi have?

12 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Twin Crescent (17 February 2026).

Which Hanabi skins can you buy now?

Field Op (749 diamonds), V.E.N.O.M. Nephila (899 diamonds), Viper (599 diamonds), Resplendent Iris (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Hanabi skin?

Vessel of Rage, Grand class, Soul Vessels series. Released in the Soul Vessels event. Limited skins come back in event draws from time to time.
