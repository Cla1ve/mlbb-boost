## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Johnson have?

8 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Sovereign of Realms (20 January 2025).

Which Johnson skins can you buy now?

Jeepney Racer (599 diamonds), S.A.B.E.R. Automata (899 diamonds), Fire Chief (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Johnson skin?

Sovereign of Realms, Supreme class, Legend series. Exchanged for 200 Magic Crystals from the Magic Wheel.
