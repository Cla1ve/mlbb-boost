## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Natan have?

7 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Sanguine Steward (24 February 2026).

Which Natan skins can you buy now?

Temporal Vortex (749 diamonds), Chaos Hunter (599 diamonds), Time Wielder (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Natan skin?

Tidal Lord, Exquisite class, Collector series. The top prize of a Collector event draw, paid with diamonds. Limited skins come back in event draws from time to time.
