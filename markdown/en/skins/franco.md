How many skins does Franco have?

Which Franco skins can you buy now?

Quantum Vanguard (749 diamonds), Blazing Axe (899 diamonds), Wheatfield Nightmare (749 diamonds), Masterchef (749 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Franco skin?

King of Hell, Supreme class, Legend series. Exchanged for 200 Magic Crystals from the Magic Wheel.
