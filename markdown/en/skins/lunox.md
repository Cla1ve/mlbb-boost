## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Lunox have?

10 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Quantum Polarity (26 September 2025).

Which Lunox skins can you buy now?

Quantum Polarity (749 diamonds), Bloody Mary (269 diamonds), Dawn Revelation (899 diamonds), Eyes of Eternity (899 diamonds), Cosmic Harmony (599 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Lunox skin?

Divine Goddess, Supreme class, Legend series. Exchanged for 200 Magic Crystals from the Magic Wheel.
