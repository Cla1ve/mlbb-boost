## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Alucard have?

10 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Demon's Bane (19 March 2025).

Which Alucard skins can you buy now?

Lightborn - Striker (1,089 diamonds), Lone Hero (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Alucard skin?

Obsidian Blade, Supreme class, Legend series. Exchanged for 200 Magic Crystals from the Magic Wheel.
