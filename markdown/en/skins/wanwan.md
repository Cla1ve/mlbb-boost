## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Wanwan have?

8 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Summer Splash (7 July 2025).

Which Wanwan skins can you buy now?

Summer Splash (749 diamonds), E-girl (749 diamonds), Shoujo Commander (599 diamonds), Darting Star (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Wanwan skin?

Flying Swallow, Grand class, Double 11 series. Released in the skin shop during an event. Limited skins come back in event draws from time to time.
