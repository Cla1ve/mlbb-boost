## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Angela have?

11 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Strings of Fate (7 February 2025).

Which Angela skins can you buy now?

Scream Doll (749 diamonds), V.E.N.O.M. Vespid (899 diamonds), Summer Vibes (749 diamonds), Dove&Love (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Angela skin?

Strings of Fate, Grand class, Kishin Densetsu series. Released in the Kishin Densetsu event. Limited skins come back in event draws from time to time.
