## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Freya have?

9 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Galactic Vanquisher (6 January 2024).

Which Freya skins can you buy now?

S.A.B.E.R. Manhunter (899 diamonds), Raven Shogun (899 diamonds), Gladiator (599 diamonds), Beach Sweetheart (749 diamonds), Dark Rose (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Freya skin?

Galactic Vanquisher, Supreme class, Legend series. Exchanged for 200 Magic Crystals from the Magic Wheel.
