## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Kadita have?

6 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Maiden of the Tide (1 August 2025).

Which Kadita skins can you buy now?

Heart of the Sea (899 diamonds), Hydromancer (749 diamonds), White Robin (749 diamonds), Atlantean Princess (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Kadita skin?

Heart of the Sea, Deluxe class, Create series. Sold in the skin shop for 899 diamonds.
