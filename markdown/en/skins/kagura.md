## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Kagura have?

11 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Lady Parasol (17 September 2025).

Which Kagura skins can you buy now?

Summer Festival (749 diamonds), Cherry Witch (749 diamonds), Flower Season (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Kagura skin?

Exorcist Kagura, Grand class, The Exorcists series. Released in the The Exorcists event. Limited skins come back in event draws from time to time.
