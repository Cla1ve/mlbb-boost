## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Claude have?

11 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Mecha Dragon (3 April 2025).

Which Claude skins can you buy now?

Blazing Trace (899 diamonds), Lifeguard (749 diamonds), Golden Bullet (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Claude skin?

Cosmic Blaze, Grand class, PRIME series. Released in the PRIME event. Limited skins come back in event draws from time to time.
