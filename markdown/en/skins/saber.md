## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Saber have?

8 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Iron Hound (1 February 2023).

Which Saber skins can you buy now?

S.A.B.E.R. Regulator (899 diamonds), Fullmetal Ronin (599 diamonds), Golden Flash (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Saber skin?

Codename - Storm, Supreme class, Legend series. Exchanged for 200 Magic Crystals from the Magic Wheel.
