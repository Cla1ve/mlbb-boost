## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Faramis have?

5 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Enigmatic Wayfarer (18 June 2025).

Which Faramis skins can you buy now?

Dark Necro (269 diamonds), Summer Sparks (749 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Faramis skin?

Infernal Magister, Deluxe class, Mythic 2023 series. Price at release: 40 Myth Coins. Not sold in the shop now: it comes back only in events.
