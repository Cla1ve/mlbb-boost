## Frequently asked questions

How many skins does Edith have?

5 skins, the default look not counted. The newest is Prismatic Sentinel (3 April 2025).

Which Edith skins can you buy now?

Prismatic Sentinel (749 diamonds), Celestial Safeguard (269 diamonds). The other skins were limited and come back only in events.

What is the rarest Edith skin?

Red Bastion, Exquisite class, Collector series. The top prize of a Collector event draw, paid with diamonds. Limited skins come back in event draws from time to time.
