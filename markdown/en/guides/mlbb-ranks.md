## How stars work in Mythic

Mythic starts after Legend I. Stars accumulate without divisions and the rank name changes at thresholds: 25 stars is Mythical Honor, 50 is Mythical Glory and 100 is Mythical Immortal. The count continues past 100 without a cap, so orders such as Immortal 100 → 150 are common.

The first Mythic matches are placement: 10 games where a win gives two stars and a loss removes none. Read more in the [Mythic placement guide](https://boostmlbb.ru/en/guides/mythic-placement.html).

## Star protection and star raising points

From Epic upward a loss sometimes triggers star protection and no star is removed. The calculator assumes this happens about once in six losses. In Epic and Legend star raising points also build up: good matches fill a bar that grants an extra star roughly every 35–50 games. Moonton does not publish exact figures, so these are estimates rather than game rules.

## How many matches it takes to reach Mythic and above

Win rate matters most. At 50% wins each Mythic star costs about 12 matches, at 55% about 6 and at 60% about 4. A couple of percent turns a hundred matches into three hundred.

## Frequently asked questions

What are the Mobile Legends ranks in order?

Warrior, Elite, Master, Grandmaster, Epic, Legend, Mythic, Mythical Honor, Mythical Glory and Mythical Immortal — 10 ranks in total.

How many stars do you need to reach Mythic?

112 stars from Warrior III: 9 in Warrior, 12 in Elite, 16 in Master and 25 each in Grandmaster, Epic and Legend.

How many stars are Mythical Glory and Immortal?

Mythical Glory starts at 50 Mythic stars and Mythical Immortal at 100. Mythical Honor starts at 25.

How many matches are in Mythic placement?

10 matches. A win gives two stars and a loss removes none, so placement can add up to 10 stars.

How many matches does it take to go from Mythical Glory to Immortal?

That is 50 stars. At a 55% win rate it is about 290 matches, at 52% about 420. The calculator on this page estimates it for your own win rate.
