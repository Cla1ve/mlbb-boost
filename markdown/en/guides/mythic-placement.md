## How placement works

1.  01

    ### Entering Mythic

    After Legend I the next win opens Mythic and placement.

2.  02

    ### 10 matches

    A win gives two stars; a loss uses a match but removes no stars.

3.  03

    ### Then one at a time

    After 10 matches stars again change by one per win or loss.

## Frequently asked questions

How many matches are in Mythic placement?

10 matches. A win gives two stars and a loss removes none.

How many stars can placement give?

Up to 10 Mythic stars — five wins.

How much is a placement boost?

It is priced per win: 165 ₽ for an account boost and 335 ₽ in a party. The calculator shows the total for the matches you have played.
