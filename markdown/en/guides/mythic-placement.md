# Mythic placement in Mobile Legends

Mythic placement is the first 10 matches after Legend I. A win gives two stars and a loss removes none, so placement can add up to 10 stars. A placement boost is priced per win: 165 ₽ for an account boost.

## How placement works

-   There are 10 placement matches; up to 5 double-star wins count toward the order price.
-   A loss uses a placement match but does not remove stars.
-   After 10 matches stars again change by one per win or loss.

## Placement rate

The price is per win. Account, party, role and hero boosts have different rates; see the full table on the [Mythic boost](https://boostmlbb.ru/en/boost/mythic.html) page and the [prices page](https://boostmlbb.ru/en/prices.html).

## What to enter in the calculator

-   Your current rank and stars.
-   Whether placement is finished; if not, how many matches you played and how many you won.
-   Your target rank and boost format.

The calculator checks that your stars and played matches agree and tells you if they do not.

## If you only need to enter Mythic

From Legend, choose Mythic with zero stars as the goal: placement is then not part of the order. To go higher, enter the stars you need and the calculator adds the placement wins.

## How many matches it takes on your own

Placement is the fastest part of Mythic. After it, at a 52% win rate a star costs 8–9 matches on average. Estimate your own route in the [match calculator](https://boostmlbb.ru/en/guides/mlbb-ranks.html).
